🔥 One-Tap & Drag Tips

  • Start with the General and Red Dot sensitivity values below.
  • Practice short upward drags in the training ground.
  • Adjust fire button size in the game's own HUD settings.
  • Use the lowest graphics option that keeps gameplay smooth.
  • Close unnecessary background apps before playing.
  • Take a break if the phone gets unusually hot.
